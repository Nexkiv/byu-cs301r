## Part 1: Art and Generative AI

As an art enthusiast, I am commonly surrounded by the opinions of artists. As mentioned in our in-class discussion,
artists have a lot of opinions. As a matter of example is Don Rosa. He is the artist credited for faithfully continuing
Carl Barks's Disney Duck comic work. He was a contemporary of the creation of the 1987 DuckTales TV show which also was
bsed heavily on Carl Barks's work. Although in recent years he now claims that it was "the best cartoon on television at
the time," he has historically fought against adaptations of Scrooge McDuck and his nephews that are not in line with
what he sees as Carl Barks's intended vision. I bring up this example to illustrate the point that artists already are
willing to fight over opinions.

Now, the interesting thing is that every artist I have interacted with all unanimously agree that using AI for the cold
generation of images is wrong and an afront to what it means to be an artist. These are traditional paint artists,
pencil sketch artists, and digital artists. There are some artists who think that some use of AI in the thinking process
or for conceptualization process is okay, but those people are always in danger of people in the art community roasting
them. Another example, I am an active participant in the Professor Layton fan community, and on April Fools' Day,
Level-5, the studio that makes Professor Layton, made a joke amusement park video post using AI. This resulted in
massive backlash from the fans. It was surprising how mad these people were getting at something that was meant to be a
light-hearted prank.

My personal opinion is that AI should not be used for image generation in any form. I hold this opinion because every
piece of AI art I have seen up until this point has had a some odd soulless feel to it for me. Although, to be fair, I
am very picky about the art I consume in general. If you ask my roommates they will tell you that I am willing to spend
5 hours looking for a specific art piece whether it exists or not. I also am picky about art styles and forms of
expression in art that I choose to surround myself with.

I strive to be accountable in my art usage. I make sure to cite the original artists of works and to give them proper
credit. I also explain to people where I get my art from and what tools I have used to modify it if I have done so. When
I did some digging into image content accountability that is what saw is the recommendation. I do though usually remove
watermarks, but I provide a description saying that the image was edited using AI. I'm not sure how truly accountable
that is. I do not cold generate artwork.
